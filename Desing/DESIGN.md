## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the legacy, technical mastery, and refined precision of a premium architectural glass and facade fabricator. With over 65 years of heritage, the brand bridges industrial craftsmanship with contemporary structural architecture. 

The aesthetic marries corporate modernity with high-end architectural minimalism, punctuated by glass-inspired tactile materiality. It evokes total confidence, engineering precision, clarity, and uncompromising structural security. 

Key visual principles:
- **Luminosity and Clarity:** Deeply expansive white spaces paired with ice-blue atmospheric washes evoke the feeling of standing in a sunlit, double-height curtain-walled space.
- **Architectural Framing:** Crisp, razor-sharp 1px aluminum lines reflect mullions, structural silicone glazing, and clean structural profiles.
- **Translucency & Frosting:** Tactile frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter`) subtly ground foreground elements over high-resolution imagery of architectural installations.
- **Commercial Authority:** Heritage credibility markers, technical specifications, and decisive conversion actions command trust through weighted typographic hierarchy and deep naval blue contrast.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in architectural glass optics, daylight dispersion, and industrial metals.

- **Primary (`#0D2137` — Structural Navy):** Represents stability, steel structural reinforcement, and 65+ years of institutional authority. Used for high-emphasis headlines, deep solid buttons, and anchoring structural surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` — Sky Glaze):** A crisp, luminous architectural blue reflecting clear sky reflections on low-iron glass facades. Deployed for primary interactive states, active indicators, and high-conversion accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E0F2FE` — Ice Crystal):** A delicate, low-saturation tint providing crisp atmospheric separation for pill badges, metric highlights, and subtle container fills.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` — Anodized Steel Slate):** An industrial balanced slate conveying the exact precision of aluminum profiles, technical subheadings, and secondary metadata.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp, ultra-bright architectural off-white.
- **Surface Elevation 0 (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crystal white for cards, tables, and hero containers.
- **Translucent Frosted Surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`):** Layered with backdrop blur for floating navigation bars, modal overlays, and heritage badges.
- **Mullion Border (`#E2E8F0` / `rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.08)`):** Precision 1px division lines reminiscent of structural window framework.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and branding with **Inter** for operational rigor and technical documentation.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Headlines):** Modern, geometric, yet refined with sculptural curves that mimic precision-bent glass and premium architectural finishes. Tight negative tracking gives large titles a confident corporate presence.
- **Inter (Body, Forms, and Data Tables):** Provides crystal-clear legibility across architectural specifications (e.g., U-values, acoustic decibel insulation, tempered thickness mm, and quote builders).
- **Eyebrow Style:** Uppercase typography with intentional character tracking (`0.12em`) set in Sky Glaze or Slate, anchoring heritage markers and product categories above primary titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column architectural grid system with strict adherence to structural alignment.

- **Container Framework:** Max content width of `80rem` (1280px) ensures layout stability on wide monitors, flanked by generous peripheral space.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, dynamic section padding scaling between `4.5rem` and `6rem`. Generous whitespace gives project showcases room to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px edge margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and page margins. Stacks architectural specs and comparison grids into horizontal swipe panels or clean vertical cascades.
- **Rhythm & Sectioning:** Large vertical divisions are separated with crisp 1px borders rather than heavy tonal fills, reinforcing the mullion-and-glass structural metaphor.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through glass refraction, sharp architectural edge borders, and ambient light distribution rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Surface Level 0 (Base):** Unpainted light canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Flat structural base.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module):** Crystal White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-diffused, cool-tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 33, 55, 0.08)` and border highlighted with Sky Glaze tint (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`).
- **Glassmorphic Surface (Headers, Badges, Overlays):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)` and a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Creates the tangible impression of high-clarity laminated safety glass.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape geometry. 

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form inputs, technical specification tags, and data grid cells. Mimics the subtle chamfered safety edge of polished architectural glass.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Architectural window modules and project cards retain crisp structural integrity without feeling aggressively sharp or playfully rounded.
- **Pill Badges (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro-metrics, and the hallmark **+65 años de experiencia** heritage pill badge.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion CTAs
- **Primary High-Conversion CTA ("Cotizar proyecto"):** Solid Deep Navy (`#0D2137`) background with white text, 0.5rem radius, padding `14px 28px`. On hover: lifts seamlessly with Sky Glaze background (`#0284C7`) and subtle ambient glow `0 8px 24px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.28)`.
- **Secondary Conversion CTA ("Hablar con un asesor"):** Transparent fill with a 1.5px structural border in `#0D2137`, Navy text, and frosted backing. On hover: shifts to Tertiary Ice tint (`#E0F2FE`) with crisp Navy text.
- **Tertiary Action:** Borderless Sky Glaze (`#0284C7`) link with an arrow glyph that translates 4px horizontally on hover.

### Heritage & Credibility Badges
- **Badge ("+65 años de experiencia"):** Frosted ice pill (`rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.7)`) with backdrop-blur, 1px border in Sky Glaze (`#BAE6FD`), deep navy bold text, and a polished metallic-inspired star or seal icon. Conveys longevity and guaranteed safety compliance.

### Form Inputs & Quote Request Selectors
- **Inputs & Dropdowns:** 44px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px corner radius. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border in Sky Glaze (`#0284C7`) along with a crisp 3px outer ring in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`. No muddy default outlines.

### Architectural Spec & Project Cards
- **Project Showcase Card:** High-contrast architectural photograph container with a 4:3 or 16:9 ratio. Overlaid glassmorphic pill badge in bottom left displaying glazing type (e.g., "Doble Vidriado Hermético - DVH").
- **Specs & Matrix Card:** Pure white surface, 1px aluminum border (`#E2E8F0`), high-contrast numeric metrics in Plus Jakarta Sans 28px navy, paired with uppercase slate metric labels (e.g., "Aislamiento Térmico", "Control Solar").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection States:** Custom 18px square (checkbox) or circular (radio) controls. Unselected: 1.5px `#94A3B8` border on white. Selected: Solid Sky Glaze (`#0284C7`) with crisp white checkmark or center pip.
